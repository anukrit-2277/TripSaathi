# Manali — The Valley of the Gods

## Overview
Manali is a hill station in Himachal Pradesh nestled in the Kullu Valley at an altitude of 2,050 meters. It's a gateway to adventure sports, the Rohtang Pass, and Ladakh. Popular with honeymooners, backpackers, and adventure seekers. The town sits on the banks of the Beas River and is surrounded by snow-capped peaks and dense deodar forests.

**Best Time to Visit**:
- **Summer (March-June)**: Pleasant weather (10-25°C). Best for sightseeing and adventure sports. Peak tourist season.
- **Monsoon (July-September)**: Heavy rainfall, landslides possible. Avoid if possible.
- **Winter (October-February)**: Snowfall in December-February. Temperature drops to -5°C. Great for snow activities. Rohtang Pass closed.
- **For Snow**: Late December to early February.

**Getting There**:
- By Air: Bhuntar Airport (KUU), 50 km from Manali. Limited flights from Delhi (1.5 hours, ₹3000-6000). Taxi from airport to Manali: ₹1500-2000.
- By Bus: From Delhi — HRTC Volvo buses (₹1000-1500, 14-16 hours overnight). Private Volvo: ₹1200-1800. Book on HRTC or RedBus.
- By Road: ~530 km from Delhi (12-14 hours by car). Via Chandigarh-Mandi route.

## Major Attractions

### Hadimba Temple (Dhungri Temple)
- **Location**: Dhungri Forest, 2 km from Mall Road
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (May-October), 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (November-April)
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: 1553 wooden temple dedicated to Hadimba Devi (from Mahabharata). Surrounded by tall deodar cedar trees. Unique pagoda-style architecture with intricate wood carvings.
- **Photography**: The wooden temple against the forest backdrop is beautiful. Best in morning light.
- **Tips**: Dress up in traditional Himachali outfits for photos (₹50-100 from local vendors). Avoid weekends — very crowded.

### Solang Valley
- **Location**: 13 km from Manali
- **Entry Fee**: Free (activities charged separately)
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Duration**: Half day (3-4 hours)
- **Highlights**: Adventure sports hub. Available activities:
  - **Paragliding**: ₹1500-2500 (10-15 minutes)
  - **Zorbing**: ₹500-800
  - **Skiing** (winter only): ₹500-1000 per session
  - **Gondola/Ropeway**: ₹500-800 return
  - **Snow Activities** (winter): Tube sliding ₹200-300
- **Tips**: Go early morning to avoid crowds. Negotiate prices for activities. Don't pay in advance to touts in Manali.

### Rohtang Pass
- **Location**: 51 km from Manali, at 3,978 meters altitude
- **Entry Fee**: Permit required — ₹550 per vehicle (book online at rohtangpermits.nic.in). Limited permits per day.
- **Timings**: Open June to October/November (weather-dependent). Closed in winter.
- **Duration**: Full day trip (6-8 hours round trip)
- **Highlights**: Stunning high-altitude pass. Snow year-round. Panoramic views of Pir Panjal range. Gateway to Lahaul-Spiti Valley.
- **Activities at Rohtang**: Snow tubing (₹200-300), skiing (₹500-800), ATV ride (₹300-500).
- **Tips**: Book permits 1-2 days in advance — they sell out. Carry warm clothes even in summer. Altitude sickness possible — carry water, move slowly. Start by 7 AM to avoid traffic jams.
- **Transport**: Taxi from Manali: ₹2000-3000 return. Don't rely on personal vehicles — road is challenging.

### Old Manali
- **Location**: 3 km from Mall Road
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours
- **Highlights**: Backpacker hub with cafés, shops, and a relaxed vibe. Stone houses, apple orchards, and the Manu Temple. Bohemian atmosphere — very different from commercial Manali.
- **Best Cafés**: Drifters' Inn, Lazy Dog Lounge, Dylan's Toasted & Roasted, 1947 Café.
- **Tips**: Walk from Mall Road to Old Manali along the Manalsu stream — beautiful 30-minute walk.

### Jogini Waterfall
- **Location**: 3 km trek from Vashisht village
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours (including trek)
- **Highlights**: 150-foot waterfall through dense forest. Moderate trek through apple orchards and pine forests. Natural pool at the base.
- **Tips**: Wear trekking shoes. Take a local guide if unfamiliar (₹200-300). Don't trek in rain — slippery trails.

### Vashisht Hot Springs & Temple
- **Location**: 3 km from Manali, Vashisht village
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM (hot springs)
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: Natural hot sulfur springs — believed to have medicinal properties. Ancient stone temple dedicated to Sage Vashisht. Separate bathing areas for men and women.
- **Tips**: Carry towel and change of clothes. Water is very hot — mix with cold water.

### Naggar Castle
- **Location**: 21 km from Manali, Naggar village
- **Entry Fee**: ₹25 for Indians
- **Timings**: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Duration**: 1-2 hours
- **Highlights**: 500-year-old castle converted to heritage hotel (HPTDC). European and Himalayan architectural blend. Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery nearby (₹50, Russian painter who lived in Naggar).
- **Tips**: Have lunch at the castle restaurant — great views and affordable (₹200-400).

### Mall Road
- **Location**: Center of Manali
- **Duration**: 1-2 hours
- **Highlights**: Main shopping and dining area. Tibetan handicrafts, woolen shawls, wooden artifacts. Street food vendors. Lively in evenings.
- **Shopping**: Himachali shawls (₹500-3000), Tibetan artifacts, local honey (₹300-500), dried fruits.

## Food & Restaurants

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Siddu**: Steamed wheat bread stuffed with poppy seeds or walnuts. Local specialty. ₹50-80.
- **Trout Fish**: Fresh river trout, grilled or fried. ₹250-400. Manali specialty.
- **Thukpa**: Tibetan noodle soup. Perfect for cold weather. ₹100-150.
- **Momos**: Steamed or fried dumplings. Available everywhere. ₹60-100.
- **Dham**: Traditional Himachali feast. Rice, dal, rajma, curd, sweet rice. ₹150-250.

### Recommended Restaurants
- **Johnson's Café**: Best continental food in Manali. Trout a speciality. ₹400-700 per person.
- **Chopsticks**: Asian cuisine in Old Manali. ₹200-400.
- **Casa Bella Vista**: Italian in Old Manali. Wood-fired pizza. ₹300-500.
- **Drifters' Inn**: Café vibes, good breakfast. Old Manali. ₹150-300.
- **Café 1947**: Live music, great food. Old Manali. ₹200-400.
- **Lazy Dog Lounge**: Cozy café with views. Old Manali. ₹200-350.
- **Il Forno**: Authentic Italian. Pizza and pasta. ₹250-450.

## Important Tips
1. Carry warm clothes even in summer — evenings are cold (10-15°C).
2. Book Rohtang Pass permits online in advance — limited daily quota.
3. Avoid driving on Rohtang road yourself if inexperienced — hire a local driver.
4. Old Manali has better cafés and vibe than Mall Road for backpackers.
5. Bargain at Mall Road shops — prices are inflated for tourists.
6. Check road conditions before planning if visiting in monsoon or early winter.
7. Altitude can affect some people — stay hydrated and rest on arrival day.
8. Try the local apple products — apple juice, apple cider, dried apples.
9. ATMs can run out of cash on weekends — carry sufficient cash.
