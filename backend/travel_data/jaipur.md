# Jaipur — The Pink City

## Overview
Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan and known as the "Pink City" due to the terracotta-pink color of its old city buildings. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, it is India's first planned city. Jaipur forms part of the famous Golden Triangle tourist circuit along with Delhi and Agra.

**Best Time to Visit**: October to March (winter season). Summers (April-June) are extremely hot with temperatures reaching 45°C. Monsoon (July-September) brings moderate rainfall.

**Getting There**:
- By Air: Jaipur International Airport (JAI), 13 km from city center. Flights from Delhi (1 hour), Mumbai (2 hours), Bangalore (2.5 hours).
- By Train: Jaipur Junction railway station. Rajdhani Express from Delhi takes ~4.5 hours. Shatabdi Express takes ~5 hours. Ticket cost: ₹500-1500 depending on class.
- By Road: ~270 km from Delhi via NH48, approximately 5-6 hours by car/bus. Volvo buses cost ₹600-1000.

## Major Attractions

### Amber Fort (Amer Fort)
- **Location**: 11 km from city center, Amer town
- **Entry Fee**: ₹100 for Indians, ₹500 for foreigners. Light & Sound show: ₹300
- **Timings**: 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, daily (Light & Sound show at 7:00 PM)
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours recommended
- **Highlights**: Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), Diwan-i-Aam, Ganesh Pol, stunning Mughal-Rajput architecture. Elephant rides available (₹1100 per person, morning only) but walking is recommended.
- **Photography**: Excellent. Best light in early morning. The fort overlooking Maota Lake is iconic.
- **Tips**: Go early (before 10 AM) to avoid crowds and heat. Wear comfortable shoes — lots of walking uphill. Hire a local guide (₹300-500) for historical context.
- **Transport from City Center**: Auto-rickshaw ₹200-300, Uber/Ola ₹150-200.

### Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)
- **Location**: Heart of the old city, Badi Choupad
- **Entry Fee**: ₹50 for Indians, ₹200 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: 953 small windows (jharokhas) designed to allow royal women to observe street life. 5 stories of red and pink sandstone. Best photographed from across the street.
- **Photography**: The exterior is most photogenic. Best photographed from the Wind View Café across the street in the morning. Instagram-worthy spot.
- **Tips**: The view from the top floor is worth the climb. Visit early morning for best photos and fewer crowds.

### City Palace
- **Location**: Tulsi Marg, near Hawa Mahal (5-minute walk)
- **Entry Fee**: ₹200 for Indians, ₹700 for foreigners. Additional ₹2500 for Royal Grandeur tour with exclusive areas.
- **Timings**: 9:30 AM to 5:00 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 hours
- **Highlights**: Mix of Rajasthani and Mughal architecture. Chandra Mahal (still royal residence), Mubarak Mahal, Diwan-i-Khas with world's largest sterling silver vessels (Guinness Record). Royal textile and arms galleries.
- **Photography**: Peacock Gate and courtyard are must-photograph spots.
- **Tips**: Audio guide available (₹200). Combine with Hawa Mahal visit — they're 5 minutes apart.

### Jantar Mantar (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- **Location**: Near City Palace (5-minute walk)
- **Entry Fee**: ₹50 for Indians, ₹200 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 45 minutes to 1 hour
- **Highlights**: World's largest stone sundial (27m tall). Collection of 19 astronomical instruments built in 1734. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2010.
- **Photography**: The geometric instruments make excellent abstract photography subjects.
- **Tips**: Hire a guide (₹200-300) — the instruments are hard to understand without explanation.

### Nahargarh Fort
- **Location**: Aravalli Hills, 6 km from city center
- **Entry Fee**: ₹50 for Indians, ₹200 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 hours
- **Highlights**: Panoramic views of Jaipur city. Madhavendra Bhawan with interconnected suites. Popular sunset viewpoint. Appears in Bollywood film "Rang De Basanti."
- **Photography**: Best at sunset. The panoramic city view is spectacular.
- **Tips**: The road up is winding — taxi recommended (₹400-500 return). The Padao restaurant on the way has great views. Combine with Jaigarh Fort (2 km away).

### Jaigarh Fort
- **Location**: Aravalli Hills, adjacent to Amber Fort
- **Entry Fee**: ₹100 for Indians, ₹200 for foreigners (combined ticket with Amber Fort available)
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: Houses Jaivana — the world's largest wheeled cannon. Connected to Amber Fort by underground passage. Military architecture, armory museum.
- **Tips**: Can be combined with Amber Fort visit — 2 km apart.

### Albert Hall Museum
- **Location**: Ram Niwas Garden, near New Gate
- **Entry Fee**: ₹40 for Indians, ₹300 for foreigners. Night viewing (7-10 PM): ₹100
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM daily
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: Oldest museum in Rajasthan. Indo-Saracenic architecture. Egyptian mummy, collection of carpets, ivory, stone, metal sculptures, and paintings.
- **Photography**: The building is beautifully lit at night — great for photography.

### Jal Mahal (Water Palace)
- **Location**: Man Sagar Lake, on the way to Amber Fort
- **Entry Fee**: Free (can only view from outside — the palace is in the middle of the lake)
- **Timings**: Viewable anytime, best at sunset
- **Duration**: 15-20 minutes
- **Photography**: Stunning at sunset with the palace reflected in the lake.

## Markets & Shopping

### Johari Bazaar
- Oldest market in Jaipur, famous for jewelry, textiles, and handicrafts.
- Open 10 AM to 9 PM. Bargain heavily — start at 50% of quoted price.

### Bapu Bazaar
- Rajasthani textiles, mojari shoes (traditional Rajasthani footwear), and souvenirs.
- Great for affordable shopping.

### Tripolia Bazaar
- Lac bangles, brassware, and traditional items. Less touristy, more authentic.

## Food & Restaurants

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Dal Baati Churma**: Rajasthan's signature dish. Baked wheat balls with lentil curry and sweet crushed wheat. ₹150-250 per plate.
- **Laal Maas**: Spicy red mutton curry, a Rajasthani non-veg specialty. ₹300-450.
- **Pyaaz Kachori**: Deep-fried onion-filled pastry. Best at Rawat Mishthan Bhandar. ₹30-50 each.
- **Ghewar**: Traditional Rajasthani sweet dessert. ₹100-200 per piece.
- **Mirchi Vada**: Stuffed chili fritters, Jaipur's favorite street snack. ₹20-30 each.

### Recommended Restaurants
- **LMB (Laxmi Mishtan Bhandar)**: Heritage restaurant in Johari Bazaar. Famous for Rajasthani thali (₹350-500). Operating since 1727.
- **Rawat Mishthan Bhandar**: Best pyaaz kachori in Jaipur. ₹30 per piece. Always crowded — worth the wait.
- **1135 AD**: Fine dining inside Amber Fort. Royal Rajasthani cuisine. ₹1500-2500 per person. Stunning ambiance.
- **Tapri Central**: Modern café with chai varieties. Great views. ₹100-200.
- **Suvarna Mahal at Rambagh Palace**: Ultra-luxury dining. ₹3000+ per person.
- **Niros**: Iconic restaurant since 1949 on MI Road. Multi-cuisine. ₹300-600 per person.

### Street Food Areas
- **MI Road**: Kachori, samosa, and chai. Budget: ₹100-150 for a full meal.
- **Masala Chowk**: Government-built food court near Ram Niwas Garden. Clean, affordable. ₹150-250 per person.
- **Chokhi Dhani**: Rajasthani village-themed resort (20 km from city). Cultural performances + dinner buffet. ₹800-1200 per person.

## Local Transportation
- **Auto-Rickshaw**: Most common. ₹10 per km. Always negotiate or insist on meter. Typical ride within city: ₹50-150.
- **Uber/Ola**: Available and reliable. Usually 20-30% cheaper than auto-rickshaws.
- **City Bus**: Jaipur City Transport Service. ₹10-20 per ride. Limited routes.
- **Cycle Rickshaw**: In old city area only. ₹30-50 per short ride.
- **Full Day Taxi**: ₹1500-2000 for 8 hours / 80 km (sedan). Recommended for fort visits.

## Important Tips
1. Bargain everywhere in markets — start at 40-50% of the quoted price.
2. Carry water bottles — Jaipur gets very hot, even in winter afternoons.
3. Wear comfortable walking shoes — forts involve significant walking.
4. Book Amber Fort visit early morning to avoid tourist bus crowds.
5. Beware of gem scam — strangers offering to show you "special" gem shops.
6. Pink City old area has traffic restrictions during peak hours.
7. Photography is allowed at most monuments but some charge extra for professional cameras (₹100-200).
8. Keep ₹50 and ₹100 notes for entry tickets — exact change helps.
