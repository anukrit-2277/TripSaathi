# Udaipur — The City of Lakes

## Overview
Udaipur, often called the "Venice of the East" or the "City of Lakes," is one of India's most romantic cities. Founded in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II, it sits in the Aravalli Range beside Lake Pichola. Known for its stunning palaces, lakes, temples, and vibrant culture.

**Best Time to Visit**: September to March. Monsoon (July-August) makes the lakes full and lush — also beautiful. Summers (April-June) are hot but less crowded, temperatures reach 40°C.

**Getting There**:
- By Air: Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR), 22 km from city center. Flights from Delhi (1.5 hours), Mumbai (1.5 hours), Jaipur (45 minutes).
- By Train: Udaipur City railway station. Chetak Express from Delhi (~12 hours, ₹400-1200). Mewar Express from Delhi (~12 hours).
- By Road: ~400 km from Jaipur (6-7 hours), ~660 km from Delhi (10-11 hours). Volvo buses from Jaipur ₹500-900.

## Major Attractions

### City Palace
- **Location**: East bank of Lake Pichola
- **Entry Fee**: ₹300 for Indians, ₹600 for foreigners. Crystal Gallery: ₹500 extra.
- **Timings**: 9:30 AM to 5:30 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours
- **Highlights**: Rajasthan's largest palace complex. Blend of Rajasthani and Mughal architecture. Balconies overlooking Lake Pichola. Courtyards, towers, terraces, and museums. Crystal Gallery houses rare Osler crystal collection.
- **Photography**: Lake views from the upper balconies are magnificent. The glass and mirror work provides excellent photo opportunities.
- **Tips**: Audio guide (₹200) is highly recommended. Start from the top and work your way down.

### Lake Pichola Boat Ride
- **Location**: Rameshwar Ghat near City Palace
- **Entry Fee**: ₹400 for Indians (1-hour shared boat), ₹800 for sunset boat ride. Private boats available.
- **Timings**: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (sunset rides at 5:00 PM)
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Highlights**: Views of City Palace, Jag Mandir, Lake Palace Hotel (now Taj Lake Palace). Sunset boat ride is the most popular.
- **Photography**: Golden hour boat ride offers the best shots of City Palace lit up.
- **Tips**: Book sunset ride 2-3 hours in advance during peak season. Evening rides fill up fast.

### Jag Mandir Island
- **Location**: In Lake Pichola (accessible by boat)
- **Entry Fee**: ₹500 including boat transfer
- **Timings**: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: 17th-century island palace. Mughal emperor Shah Jahan took refuge here. Beautiful gardens, Gul Mahal with yellow sandstone.
- **Tips**: Can be combined with Lake Pichola boat ride.

### Saheliyon Ki Bari (Garden of the Maidens)
- **Location**: Saheli Marg, north of city
- **Entry Fee**: ₹30 for Indians, ₹100 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: Beautiful gardens with fountains, kiosks, marble elephants, and lotus pool. Built for 48 royal maids who came as part of a princess's dowry.
- **Photography**: The fountains with marble elephants make beautiful shots.

### Jagdish Temple
- **Location**: City Palace Road, 150m from City Palace
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: 5:00 AM to 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM
- **Duration**: 30 minutes
- **Highlights**: Indo-Aryan architecture, dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Built in 1651. Carved pillars and 50-foot tall structure.
- **Tips**: Evening aarti (prayer ceremony) at 7:00 PM is a spiritual experience.

### Monsoon Palace (Sajjangarh)
- **Location**: On a hilltop, 5 km from city center
- **Entry Fee**: ₹80 for Indians, ₹300 for foreigners. Vehicle entry: ₹85
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours (including drive up)
- **Highlights**: Hilltop palace with panoramic views of Udaipur, lakes, and Aravalli hills. Originally built to watch monsoon clouds. Featured in James Bond film "Octopussy."
- **Photography**: Sunset views from here are Udaipur's best. Panoramic city views.
- **Tips**: Go for sunset — arrive by 5 PM. Taxi up the hill is ₹300-400 return.

### Fateh Sagar Lake
- **Location**: North of Lake Pichola
- **Entry Fee**: Free to visit. Boat ride: ₹200 per person
- **Timings**: Boating 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- **Duration**: 1 hour
- **Highlights**: Scenic lake surrounded by hills. Nehru Garden (island garden accessible by boat). Less crowded than Lake Pichola. Popular with locals for evening walks.

### Bagore Ki Haveli
- **Location**: Gangaur Ghat, near City Palace
- **Entry Fee**: ₹100 for Indians, ₹100 for foreigners. Evening show: ₹150
- **Timings**: 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Evening show at 7:00 PM (1 hour)
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours (museum), 1 hour (evening show)
- **Highlights**: 18th-century haveli turned museum. 138 rooms with period displays. Evening cultural show features Rajasthani folk dances (Ghoomar, Kalbeliya) and puppet show.
- **Tips**: Evening cultural show is a MUST — one of Udaipur's highlights. Book in advance.

## Food & Restaurants

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Dal Baati Churma**: Available everywhere, Udaipur's staple. ₹150-250.
- **Gatte Ki Sabzi**: Gram flour dumplings in spicy curry. ₹120-180.
- **Kachori**: Stuffed fried pastry. Popular breakfast item. ₹20-40.

### Recommended Restaurants
- **Ambrai Restaurant**: Lakeside dining with City Palace and Jag Mandir views. ₹500-800 per person. Best dinner spot in Udaipur.
- **Upré by 1559 AD**: Rooftop restaurant at Lake Pichola Hotel. Stunning views. ₹800-1200 per person.
- **Millets of Mewar**: Healthy millet-based cuisine. Unique concept. ₹200-400.
- **Savage Garden**: Quirky café near Jagdish Temple. Continental and Indian. ₹200-350.
- **Natraj Dining Hall**: Authentic Rajasthani thali. ₹150-250 per person. No-frills, delicious.
- **Jheel Café**: Budget lakeside café. ₹100-200.

## Local Transportation
- **Auto-Rickshaw**: ₹10 per km. Negotiate before boarding. City rides: ₹30-100.
- **Uber/Ola**: Available but limited. Usually cheaper than autos.
- **Walking**: Old city area is very walkable. Most attractions are within 2-3 km.
- **Full Day Taxi**: ₹1200-1800 for 8 hours / 80 km.

## Important Tips
1. Lake Pichola sunset boat ride is the #1 must-do — book early.
2. Old city is walkable — save on transport by staying near City Palace.
3. Evening cultural show at Bagore Ki Haveli is a must-see.
4. Udaipur is safer and more relaxed than most Indian tourist cities.
5. Carry cash — many smaller shops and attractions don't accept cards.
6. The city is hilly — comfortable shoes recommended.
7. Visit Vintage Car Collection if you're a car enthusiast (₹250, near Garden Hotel).
