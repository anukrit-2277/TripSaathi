# Delhi — The Capital City

## Overview
Delhi, India's capital, is a sprawling metropolis blending ancient history with modern urbanity. Old Delhi (Shahjahanabad) was founded by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1639, while New Delhi was designed by British architects Edwin Lutyens and Herbert Baker in 1911. The city has been the seat of power for multiple empires and dynasties.

**Best Time to Visit**: October to March. Winters (December-January) can be cold and foggy (5-15°C). Summers (April-June) are brutally hot (40-47°C). Monsoon (July-September) is humid with heavy rainfall.

**Getting There**:
- By Air: Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), major hub with domestic and international flights. Metro connected.
- By Train: New Delhi Railway Station, Old Delhi Railway Station, Nizamuddin Railway Station. Well-connected to all major cities.
- By Road: Connected to all major cities via national highways. Excellent bus connectivity.

## Major Attractions

### Red Fort (Lal Qila) — UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Location**: Netaji Subhash Marg, Old Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: ₹35 for Indians, ₹500 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, closed Mondays
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 hours
- **Highlights**: Built by Shah Jahan in 1638. Massive red sandstone walls. Diwan-i-Aam, Diwan-i-Khas, Rang Mahal. Independence Day flag hoisting site.
- **Photography**: The Lahore Gate is the iconic shot. Interior gardens and fountains are photogenic.
- **Tips**: Light and Sound show in evenings (₹80). Closed on Mondays. Go early to avoid crowds. Combined with Jama Masjid and Chandni Chowk (all walking distance).

### Qutub Minar — UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Location**: Mehrauli, South Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: ₹35 for Indians, ₹550 for foreigners
- **Timings**: 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, daily
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: 73m tall victory tower, started in 1193. India's tallest brick minaret. Intricate carvings and inscriptions. Iron Pillar of Delhi (1600+ years old, rust-free).
- **Photography**: The minaret against blue sky is stunning. Best in morning light.

### Humayun's Tomb — UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Location**: Nizamuddin East
- **Entry Fee**: ₹35 for Indians, ₹550 for foreigners
- **Timings**: Sunrise to sunset, daily
- **Duration**: 1-1.5 hours
- **Highlights**: Precursor to the Taj Mahal. Built in 1570. First garden tomb in India. Persian-influenced Mughal architecture. Beautiful symmetrical gardens (Char Bagh).
- **Photography**: The tomb reflected in the central water channel is the classic shot.
- **Tips**: Visit nearby Nizamuddin Dargah for Sufi qawwali music in the evening (free, Thursday evenings are special).

### India Gate & Kartavya Path
- **Location**: Central Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: Open 24 hours (best in evening)
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: 42m war memorial designed by Lutyens. Amar Jawan Jyoti (eternal flame). Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath) stretches to Rashtrapati Bhavan.
- **Photography**: Evening when illuminated is best. Panoramic shot with Rashtrapati Bhavan in background.
- **Tips**: Visit in the evening for the illuminated view. Ice cream and snack vendors around. Great for an evening walk.

### Jama Masjid
- **Location**: Old Delhi, near Red Fort
- **Entry Fee**: Free (camera: ₹200)
- **Timings**: 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM, 1:30 PM to 6:30 PM (closed during prayer times)
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: India's largest mosque. Built by Shah Jahan in 1656. Can hold 25,000 worshippers. Climb the southern minaret for panoramic Old Delhi views (₹100).
- **Tips**: Dress modestly. Remove shoes. Scarves available for women at entrance.

### Chandni Chowk
- **Location**: Old Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: Shops open 10 AM to 8 PM (closed Sundays)
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours for food + shopping
- **Highlights**: 350-year-old market built by Shah Jahan. Narrow lanes, bustling energy. Famous for street food, spices, electronics, wedding shopping.
- **Best Food**: Paranthe Wali Gali (₹100-200 for parantha platter), Natraj Dahi Bhalla (₹60), Old Famous Jalebi Wala (₹50-80/plate), Karim's (Mughlai non-veg, ₹200-400).

### Lotus Temple
- **Location**: Bahapur, South Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM (closed Mondays)
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Highlights**: Bahá'í House of Worship shaped like a lotus flower. 27 marble petals. Open to all faiths for meditation.
- **Photography**: Best from the gardens surrounding it.

### Akshardham Temple
- **Location**: Noida Link Road, East Delhi
- **Entry Fee**: Free (exhibition: ₹220)
- **Timings**: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM (closed Mondays)
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours
- **Highlights**: Massive modern Hindu temple. Intricately carved from pink sandstone. Musical fountain show (₹80, evening). Boat ride through India's cultural history.
- **Tips**: No phones, cameras, or bags allowed inside. Lockers available at entry (free). Plan at least 2 hours.

## Food & Restaurants

### Must-Try Delhi Foods
- **Chole Bhature**: Spiced chickpea curry with deep-fried bread. Best at Sita Ram Diwan Chand (₹80-100).
- **Butter Chicken**: Delhi's gift to the world. Invented at Moti Mahal, Daryaganj. ₹250-400.
- **Paranthas**: At Paranthe Wali Gali, Chandni Chowk. Stuffed with various fillings. ₹100-200 for a platter.
- **Kebabs**: Karim's near Jama Masjid. Operating since 1913. ₹200-400 per person.
- **Chaat**: Dahi Bhalla, Golgappa, Aloo Tikki. Best at Haldiram's or street vendors. ₹50-100.
- **Biryani**: At Karim's or Al Jawahar. ₹200-350 per plate.

### Popular Restaurants
- **Indian Accent**: India's best restaurant (Asia's Top 50). Modern Indian. ₹4000-6000 per person. Reservation essential.
- **Karim's**: Legendary Mughlai since 1913. Near Jama Masjid. ₹300-500 per person.
- **Bukhara at ITC Maurya**: Famous Dal Bukhara. ₹3000-5000 per person.
- **Saravana Bhavan**: South Indian chain. ₹150-250 per person.
- **SodaBottleOpenerWala**: Parsi café. Fun decor. ₹400-600 per person.

## Local Transportation
- **Delhi Metro**: Best way to travel. ₹10-60 per ride. Token or Metro card. Covers most tourist spots. Clean, safe, efficient.
- **Uber/Ola**: Widely available. ₹100-300 for most city rides.
- **Auto-Rickshaw**: ₹20-100 per ride. Always negotiate or use the meter.
- **Cycle Rickshaw**: In Old Delhi only. ₹30-50 per ride. Fun experience.
- **Hop-On Hop-Off Bus**: HOHO bus covers major sights. ₹500/day.

## Important Tips
1. Delhi Metro is the best way to get around — avoid road traffic during peak hours (8-10 AM, 5-8 PM).
2. Old Delhi is best explored on foot or cycle rickshaw — cars can't navigate the narrow lanes.
3. Book Akshardham visit for morning — no phones/cameras allowed.
4. Air quality can be poor in winter (November-January) — carry a mask.
5. Keep belongings secure in crowded areas — be aware of pickpockets in markets.
6. Monday closures: Red Fort, Lotus Temple, Akshardham, most museums.
7. Street food is incredible but choose busy stalls for freshness and hygiene.
